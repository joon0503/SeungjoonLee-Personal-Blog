I finished Ph. D. at Control & Dynamics System Lab at Seoul National University. After graduation, I have worked as robotics engineer in Naver LABS and currently with CarbonSix Robotics. Some of my research area and interests include

- Analysis and control of linear and nonlinear dynamical systems
- Synchronization and consensus of multi-agent systems
- Distributed optimization / Optimization algorithms as a continuous dynamical system and its implication for discrete-time dynamics
- Reinforcement learning from the control perspective
- Computer vision and applications to robotics
- Machine learning approaches to manipulation including imitation learning, vision-language action models, and world models
